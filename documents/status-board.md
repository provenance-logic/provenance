**The session also surfaced a deeper issue** when verifying `demo-reset.sh --soft` end-to-end after bringing the demo box to current main: both `softReset` (column/table references that don't exist) and `demo-smoke-test.sh` layers 3–6 (flat top-level endpoints that don't exist on the multi-tenant API) have been broken since they were written. The platform itself works; the operator's *view* into whether the platform works has drifted. Filed as [B-060](bugs/open.md#B-060). This is exactly the pattern the new "API-complete is not phase-complete" CLAUDE.md rule is designed to catch — for the operator persona, the smoke test and reset scripts *are* the surface.

**No single bug warrants the "most concerning" callout anymore.** B-060 is the largest open item by scope but it's tooling-layer, not platform-layer; doesn't block OSR.
